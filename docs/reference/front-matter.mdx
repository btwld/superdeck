# Front matter reference

A slide may open with a YAML mapping between two `---` lines. Everything after
the closing `---` is the slide's content.

```markdown
---
title: Product Vision
layout: fullscreen
owner: Platform Team
---

# Our plan for the year
```

Keys that SuperDeck does not define are still preserved, and are read through
`slide.options.args['owner']`. See the
[Markdown syntax reference](/reference/markdown-syntax#slide-front-matter) for
the supported keys.

## When a block is read as front matter

A `---` line opens front matter only when every line up to the closing `---`
belongs to a YAML mapping:

- The first non-blank line is an unquoted key, such as `title: Deck`.
- Later lines may be more mapping entries, including quoted keys such as
  `"my key": value`.
- Indented lines continue the value above them, so nested maps, block scalars
  (`|` and `>`), and indented sequences all work.
- A sequence under a key may be indented or indentless.

```markdown
---
title: Release notes
tags:
  - dart
  - flutter
owners:
- platform
summary: |
  Two lines of
  literal text
---
```

Anything else makes the whole block slide content, and the `---` lines become
ordinary slide separators:

- A bullet list with no key above it. `- First bullet` on its own is a list,
  not front matter.
- A prose line at the start of the block, or beside the mapping entries.
- A line that opens with `#`, `@`, `>` or `!`. These mark slide content
  wherever they appear, **including inside a block scalar**. Move such text
  below the front matter, or mark it with `@block`.

## Mapping-shaped prose

A lone `Key: value` line between two `---` lines is both a valid one-entry YAML
mapping and a plausible sentence. SuperDeck reads it as front matter:

```markdown
---
Note: remember this
---
```

The slide above has the option `Note` and no content. Mark the text with
`@block` when you mean it as content:

```markdown
---
@block
Note: remember this
---
```

## Errors

A block that is recognized as front matter but does not parse as a YAML map
fails the build, naming the slide's front matter. Unbalanced quotes are the
usual cause:

```markdown
---
title: "unclosed
---
```

Front matter that parses to a sequence or a scalar instead of a map is reported
the same way, rather than being silently dropped.
